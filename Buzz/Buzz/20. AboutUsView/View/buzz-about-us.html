Buzz

Become Legend

Buzz is a sports-first social platform for basketball players who love to play in the real world and connect digitally. Organize matches, record results, chat with teammates and opponents, and discover nearby courts — all from one intuitive app.

Key Features

  • Instant Messaging

    Chat live with teammates and opponents to coordinate games and share moments instantly.

  • Game Management

    Record match results, track stats, and review your past performance.

  • Find Nearby Courts

    Discover basketball courts near you and connect with players in your area.

  • Rankings & Points

    Earn points, climb leaderboards, and show off your competitive edge.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To create a digital home for athletes and basketball lovers where competition meets community.
Mission: To connect real-world players through technology, encouraging a healthy, social, and competitive basketball culture.
