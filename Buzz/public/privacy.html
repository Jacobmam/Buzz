Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: October 29, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Buzz, a basketball-first social platform for players who organize games, message teammates, and discover nearby courts. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information — name, username, email, and optional profile photo.
  • Gameplay Data — game results, rankings, and participation history.
  • Location Data — approximate location (with permission) to find nearby courts.
  • Messages — chat messages exchanged between users (visible only to participants).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve Buzz’s features and services.
  • To manage user accounts and enable messaging.
  • To help users find local courts and games.
  • To display game stats, rankings, and history.
  • To send notifications and updates (if allowed).

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your information. Data may be shared with:

  • Other Buzz users during chat or games.
  • Trusted service providers assisting in app operations.
  • Authorities if required by law.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Firebase cloud infrastructure using industry-standard protection measures. While we strive to secure your data, no digital system is entirely immune to risk.

6. Your Rights

  • Access or update your information in-app.
  • Request account deletion via support.
  • Manage notification and location permissions in device settings.

7. Policy Updates

Buzz may revise this policy occasionally. Updates will appear in-app with a new “Last updated” date.

8. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at buzzofficial.app@gmail.com.
